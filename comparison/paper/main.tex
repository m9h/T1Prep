\begin{abstract}
The release of the MedARC \texttt{smri-fm} dataset, the publication of
BrainIAC \citep{brainiac2026}, and the MICCAI 2025 FOMO25 and SSL3D
challenges won by \citet{gordaliza2026} have renewed interest in
structural-MRI foundation models (sMRI-FMs) as a general-purpose
backbone for neuroimaging. Whether FMs provide a meaningful advance
over classical morphometric features for core tasks --- above all,
brain-age prediction --- has not been settled. In fact
\citet{schulz2025} show that for \emph{disease detection}, simpler
models beat complex ones despite losing on age MAE: ridge regression
with 1\,400 parameters produces larger patient-vs-control effect sizes
than a 46M-parameter CNN on the same data. We therefore construct a
\textbf{four-rung classical morphometry ladder} on the Dallas Lifespan
Brain Study (DLBS, \citealp{park2025dlbs}) and benchmark three sMRI
foundation-model checkpoints against it under identical
bias-correction regimes. This manuscript is generated with Pweave so
every number and figure below updates as the experiment parquets land.



\textbf{Current snapshot:} 23 subjects, 60 sessions processed by FastSurfer VINN; 23 subjects, 60 sessions by T1Prep (CAT-Surface).

\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Brain-predicted age, introduced by \citet{franke2010} and popularised
by Cole and colleagues \citep{cole2017}, has become the single
most-reported biomarker derived from structural MRI. A decade of
architectural progress --- from relevance-vector regression on VBM
features, through Gaussian-process regression \citep{cole2017,brainager}
to 3D CNNs \citep{peng2021sfcn} --- has pushed UK Biobank mean absolute
error (MAE) from $\sim 5$ years to $\sim 2$ years \citep{peng2021sfcn}.
At the same time, multiple authors have shown that classical ridge- or
kernel-based regressors on FreeSurfer or CAT12 morphometric features
match deep networks within noise \citep{more2023,lombardi2021,bashyam2020}.
\citet{gijsen2024} established in a 46\,381-subject UK Biobank study
that ResNet-50 and SwinT reach an indistinguishable \SI{2.66}{years}
MAE, with SwinT scaling better only above $\sim 25$k training subjects.
The apparent ceiling is set by label noise and site effects, not by
model capacity.

\citet{schulz2025} take this further and show that for disease
detection, simpler models actually \emph{beat} complex ones on
patient-vs-control effect size even when they lose on age MAE. This
finding motivates our evaluation plan: disease-effect-size is reported
alongside age MAE for every arm.

\section{Methods (pre-registration)}

\subsection{Data}

We use the Dallas Lifespan Brain Study \citep{park2025dlbs}, OpenNeuro
\texttt{ds004856}, with 464 / 338 / 224 subjects across waves 1/2/3
($\sim 1000$ T1 sessions, Philips 3T Achieva, $\sim 4$--$5$ year
intervals, ages \numrange{20}{90} at wave 1). External generalisation
is evaluated on ds003592 \citep{spreng2022}. Both were downloaded from
OpenNeuro S3 on 2026-04-22.

\subsection{Four classical morphometry rungs}

Every pipeline runs inside a pinned Docker image from the
\texttt{ghcr.io/m9h/neurocontainers-arm} registry for reproducibility
on NVIDIA Grace-Blackwell arm64 hosts.

\begin{description}
  \item[Rung 1 --- SynthSeg volumes (MedARC baseline).] The MedARC
    \texttt{preprocessing/pipeline.py} \citep{medarcsmrifm2026}:
    SynthStrip, ANTs rigid to \texttt{MNI152NLin2009cAsym},
    \texttt{mri\_synthseg --parc --robust}. Aggregated to 71
    ICV-normalised features per \citet{bethlehem2022}. Implemented
    upstream by \citet{NimaAshtari2026}.

  \item[Rung 2 --- SynthSeg + N4.] Tests whether an upstream bias
    step helps a bias-robust segmentation network.

  \item[Rung 3 --- FastSurfer VINN.] \texttt{aparc.DKTatlas+aseg.deep}
    VINN + CerebNet + HypVINN. Isolates seg-network quality from bias
    handling. Runs in
    \texttt{ghcr.io/m9h/fastsurfer-arm:6b6b985-arm.1}.

  \item[Rung 4 --- T1Prep thickness + Jacobian.] T1Prep v0.3.3
    \citep{t1prep} (DeepMriPrep + AMAP + CAT-Surface). Adds cortical
    thickness per Desikan--Killiany ROI and nonlinear-warp log-Jacobian
    --- the two feature axes no volumetric seg captures. Runs in
    \texttt{ghcr.io/m9h/t1prep-arm:v0.3.3-arm.1}.
\end{description}

\subsection{Bias correction}

For every arm we report MAE under four regimes: raw, Cole-Smith
\citep{smith2019}, Beheshti \citep{liang2019}, and Zhang age-level
\citep{zhang2023agelevel}.

\subsection{Disease-effect-size reporting per \citet{schulz2025}}

For each arm we report patient-vs-control effect size (Cohen's~$d$)
on an AD-spectrum labelling derived from DLBS participant metadata.

\subsection{Longitudinal stability per \citet{vidalpineiro2021}}

For each returning subject we compute $\Delta\mathrm{BAG}_{i,t}$
between waves and test (a) within-subject stability (ICC), and
(b) correlation with change in morphometric summaries. DLBS's three-wave
structure is the first public venue where this test can be run on an
sMRI-FM.

\section{Infrastructure contributions}

All pipelines run on an NVIDIA DGX Spark (GB10 Grace-Blackwell, arm64).
Upstream neuroimaging containers are almost entirely amd64 and fall
back to QEMU emulation on Grace, which is unusably slow for TF/PyTorch
inference. To close this gap we contribute:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{ghcr.io/m9h/t1prep-arm:v0.3.3-arm.1} --- arm64 T1Prep
    with three Blackwell-specific patches pending upstream PR
    (deterministic-algorithm warn\_only fallback, weight-cache write
    permissions, case-sensitive Neuromorphometrics atlas filenames).
  \item \texttt{ghcr.io/m9h/fastsurfer-arm:6b6b985-arm.1} --- arm64
    FastSurfer seg\_only, NGC PyTorch 24.12 base. Published in
    response to Deep-MI/FastSurfer issue \#716.
  \item \texttt{ghcr.io/m9h/medarc-arm} (pending build) --- MedARC's
    \texttt{smri-fm/preprocessing/pipeline.py} on arm64 via locally
    built FreeSurfer 8.2.0 .debs.
\end{itemize}

Recipes + auto-build workflow at
\url{https://github.com/m9h/neurocontainers-arm}.

\section{Pre-registered hypotheses}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{Cross-sectional ceiling.} FM + ridge and rung-4 (T1Prep
    thickness + Jacobian) + ridge match within \SI{0.3}{years} MAE on
    wave-1 held-out subjects.
  \item \emph{Bias persistence.} Regression-dilution slope $\alpha$
    indistinguishable across preprocessing and FM arms.
  \item \emph{Longitudinal disconnect.} Within-subject $\Delta\mathrm{BAG}$
    correlates weakly ($|r| < 0.2$) with within-subject morphometric
    change, per \citet{vidalpineiro2021}, for every arm.
  \item \emph{Disease-effect-size inversion.} Per \citet{schulz2025},
    rungs 1--4 have higher patient-vs-control effect size than any
    FM + ridge despite losing on age MAE.
\end{enumerate}
